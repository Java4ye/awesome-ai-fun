## 1. Pelican-bicycle alternatives

- **Source**: https://gally.net/temp/20260914pelican-alternatives/index.html
- **About the site**: https://gally.net/temp/20260914pelican-alternatives/about.html
- **Site creation and funding**: The source says Claude assisted in creating the site, with Tom Gally responsible for the project and costs.
- **Indexed here**: 30 SVG drawing tasks, with source-site comparisons of outputs for the same task.

- **Context**: The source says its 2026 round currently covers only the first 10 tasks. Do not assume all 30 tasks have results from that round.

## 2. AI Test Museum

- **Source**: https://aitestmuseum.com/zh
- **Indexed here**: 20 challenges: 8 animation, 4 games, 6 3D scenes, and 2 applications.
- **Focus**: Background, full prompts, outputs, and test conditions for classic tasks.
- **Context**: The source identifies some prompts as adaptations. Its displayed results come from specific agent environments, and some tasks have been answered again. Consider those conditions when comparing outputs.
- **Terms**: https://aitestmuseum.com/zh/terms
- **Our contribution**: Direct links to individual tasks, with original summaries and observation points in Chinese and English.

## Recommending another source

Look for identifiable authors and traceable links. Prefer task names, short descriptions, observation points, and source URLs. Do not copy whole sites, model outputs, screenshots, or long prompts without permission.

When several sites list the same task, trace the earliest available source where possible and note any adaptation. See the [contribution guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.en.md).
