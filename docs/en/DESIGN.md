# Gallery design

Awesome AI Fun is a browsable catalog of real creations. The interface borrows from a printed exhibition index: warm paper, dark ink, a muted burgundy accent, thin rules, and numbered works.

- Keep all works visible by default, grouped by category. Roman numerals tie the navigation to the category headings; work numbers follow catalog order and stay the same while filtering.
- Artwork supplies the color. Preserve its proportions inside a narrow paper frame. Keep the title, creator, and play or demo link below the cover.
- Use serif type for the title and category headings, sans serif for controls and captions, and monospace for the numbers.
- Keep three columns on desktop, two at intermediate widths, and one on phones. Search and categories stay available while browsing; on phones, only the category row sticks.
- English opens by default. Chinese uses the same layout and content. Load covers lazily and play video only after the visitor opens a work.
