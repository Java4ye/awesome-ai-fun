This collection indexes traceable historical model outputs. It does not require regenerating them with unavailable models. Model names, run dates, and environments follow source records. We have not independently verified their generation, and community tests are not vendor-official demonstrations or endorsements.

Linked materials remain subject to their owners' rights and source terms. Our MIT license covers our original content and code; it does not relicense third-party media. Separately licensed material retains its own license.

## Adding more media

Prefer original creator posts, repositories, and blogs. Record the author separately from the publisher, and distinguish run dates, collection dates, and model release dates. Leave undisclosed details empty. Prefer original-site playback links for videos.

Check the particular asset's conditions before storing it, and retain the license or permission basis. Remote images, screenshots, crops, and screen recordings also need an appropriate basis. Do not substitute a similar image from another task.

## Corrections and removal

Creators and rights holders can open a [media correction / removal Issue](https://github.com/Java4ye/awesome-ai-tests/issues/new?template=media-correction-en.md) with the case ID, original work URL, affected files, and requested action. We will review attribution corrections, broken links, model-label errors, or removal requests.

Corrections and removals will record the affected cases and action taken.
